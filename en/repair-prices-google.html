Free diagnosis. Agree repair costs first.

A standard fault diagnosis is free. A written diagnostic report, condition assessment or repair-cost statement for your insurer is chargeable. We agree the fee with you before preparing the document.

Shipping to us and free return delivery

You pay for shipping to Mujitech. Mujitech pays return shipping after the repair. Please wait until we confirm the shipping address before sending your device.

What does the part quality mean?

Original Service Pack means a manufacturer service replacement part. Original Pulled means an original part removed from another device. Premium is an aftermarket part category, not a promise of an original manufacturer part; we confirm the exact screen technology and compatibility for your model.

The listed repair price includes the specified replacement part and the labour for that repair. We confirm part availability and suitability before the order. We only address additional faults or provide further services with your approval.
