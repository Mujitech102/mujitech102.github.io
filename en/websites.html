Questions before your website enquiry

Are the domain and hosting included in the package?

The domain, hosting and paid external services are not included in the website package. Any setup you need and ongoing costs are agreed separately.

Who provides the text, images and legal content?

You generally provide this content. Professional translations and legal advice are not included. We discuss any additional services before starting.

Do I need to choose a package already?

No. First describe your business and what you want your website to achieve. We then discuss the number of pages, features and content. The exact scope and final price are agreed in writing before work begins.

Is monthly website care compulsory?

No, website care is optional. You can select it in your enquiry or discuss it with us first. The agreed scope is recorded in the quotation.
