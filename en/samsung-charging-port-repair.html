Samsung Galaxy charging port repair

Repairs in Abtwil near St. Gallen and by post from across Switzerland. Device handover by appointment only; no walk-in shop.

Charging stops or only works at a certain cable angle

A loose cable or intermittent charging may involve the port. Cables, chargers, the battery, moisture and charging electronics can cause similar symptoms. On an undamaged, dry device, first try a known working compatible cable and charger. Do not put metal objects into the port.

Samsung Galaxy models can use different USB ports and charging boards. A similar-looking assembly may not fit your model variant.

Identify the fault before choosing a part

Depending on the model, the charging port may be part of a separate assembly or linked to other functions. Replacing it does not fix unrelated mainboard faults. Tell us whether data transfer, the microphone and wireless charging still work. The correct part and repair options are confirmed in advance.

How your repair works

  1. Describe the model and fault and send a repair enquiry.
  2. Mujitech confirms the part, availability, price and agreed process before paid work begins.
  3. Send the device by arrangement or hand it over at an agreed appointment. You pay shipping to Mujitech; return shipping after repair is paid by Mujitech.

Before handing over your device

Back up your data where possible. Do not send passwords or unlock codes through the chat. Additional damage and other work are agreed with you in advance.

Common questions

Could cleaning be enough?

Dirt may be involved. Inspection is needed to decide whether cleaning is enough or a part is required. The replacement price is not a standard cleaning fee.

What if there is a moisture warning?

Do not connect a cable while the device warns about moisture. Do not heat or open it. Discuss liquid damage personally before shipping.
