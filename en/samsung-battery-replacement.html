Where can I find my Galaxy model?

If the device is still usable, you can usually find the model number under Settings → About phone, or About tablet. If the screen is black, check the original packaging. The model name or number is enough for your initial enquiry – do not send an IMEI, serial number or unlock code.

Short battery life or unexpected shutdowns

Short runtime, sudden shutdowns and an ageing battery may make replacement worth considering. Apps, poor reception and other faults can also cause high consumption. Provide the model, device age and symptoms. The displayed battery capacity can help assessment, but it does not replace fault diagnosis.

Galaxy S, A and other series use different batteries and designs. Similar model names are not enough to choose a part; provide the exact model designation.

Confirming battery type and condition

Service Pack, Pulled and other replacement grades are different offers. Pulled means removed from a device and does not mean factory-new. Mujitech agrees the specific variant before an order. The model name alone cannot establish your battery condition or guarantee a particular runtime after repair.

How your repair works

  1. Describe the model and fault and send a repair enquiry.
  2. Mujitech confirms the part, availability, price and agreed process before paid work begins.
  3. Send the device by arrangement or hand it over at an agreed appointment. You pay shipping to Mujitech; return shipping after repair is paid by Mujitech.

Before handing over your device

Back up your data where possible. Do not send passwords or unlock codes through the chat. Additional damage and other work are agreed with you in advance. If your Galaxy supports Maintenance mode and is still usable, you can enable it before handing over the device. It does not replace a backup.

Common questions

Is replacing the battery in an older Samsung Galaxy worthwhile?

It depends on the device condition, other damage and repair cost. You can also enquire about older models such as Galaxy Note 9 or Galaxy S6. Provide the exact model; parts availability and repair feasibility are checked individually. A listed model does not confirm stock.

Do charging problems always mean a faulty battery?

No. The power adapter, cable, charging port or charging electronics may also be involved. Tell us whether the device charges, only works when plugged in or shuts down despite a charged battery. Diagnosis helps identify the appropriate next step.

What if the battery is swollen?

If there is swelling, excessive heat, smoke or liquid damage, stop charging and do not open the device. Discuss transport or shipping with Mujitech first.

Will my data be kept?

Battery replacement does not mean a factory reset. Nevertheless, back up important files beforehand if the device allows it. Data preservation cannot be guaranteed in every case.
