Confirming battery type and condition

Service Pack, Pulled and other replacement grades are different offers. Pulled means removed from a device and does not mean factory-new. Mujitech agrees the specific variant before an order. The model name alone cannot establish your battery condition or guarantee a particular runtime after repair.

How your repair works

  1. Describe the model and fault and send a repair enquiry.
  2. Mujitech confirms the part, availability, price and agreed process before paid work begins.
  3. Send the device by arrangement or hand it over at an agreed appointment. You pay shipping to Mujitech; return shipping after repair is paid by Mujitech.

Before handing over your device

Back up your data where possible. Do not send passwords or unlock codes through the chat. Additional damage and other work are agreed with you in advance.

Common questions

What if the battery is swollen?

If there is swelling, excessive heat, smoke or liquid damage, stop charging and do not open the device. Discuss transport or shipping with Mujitech first.

Will my data be kept?

Battery replacement does not mean a factory reset. Nevertheless, back up important files beforehand if the device allows it. Data preservation cannot be guaranteed in every case.
