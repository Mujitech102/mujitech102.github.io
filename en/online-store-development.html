Web design / Online store development

Have an online store built for your Swiss business

Want to sell products online or improve an existing store? Mujitech plans your e-commerce website around your range, customers and workflow. Receive an individual quote for the features you actually need.

Request a quote

From product catalogue to online store

We discuss product numbers and variants, the information customers need and how you want to process orders. Categories, product pages and a clear route to ordering form the foundation.

Plan payments, shipping and languages

Payment methods, delivery areas, shipping rules and language versions belong in the project scope. We check integration feasibility and provider fees before implementation. Translations, legal texts and content responsibilities are agreed separately.

What does online-store development cost?

Custom online stores are quoted on request. Product numbers, variants, data migration, languages and integrations affect the cost. The standard website packages are not fixed shop prices. Domains, hosting and paid third-party services are not automatically included.

Mobile usability and search-friendly structure

We plan clear navigation and informative product and category text. Page titles, descriptions and technically accessible content help search engines understand the range. Search positions, sales and enquiries cannot be guaranteed.

Working with businesses across Switzerland

We can coordinate by email, phone and remotely. Enquiries are welcome in German, English, French or Italian. Scope, content, responsibilities, schedule and cost are agreed before the project starts.

Common questions

What do you need to prepare a quotation?

Describe your products, approximate product count, languages and payment methods. For an existing shop, include its link and the improvements you want.

Is ongoing maintenance included?

Ongoing support and later changes are agreed separately. We discuss what you want to maintain yourself and where you need help.

Can I request a business website without a shop?

Yes. Our web-design page lists website packages, their scope and optional maintenance.
