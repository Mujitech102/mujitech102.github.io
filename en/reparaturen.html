Frequently asked questions

Questions about repairs

Which devices does Mujitech repair?

We offer repairs and technical error analysis for smartphones, iPhones, tablets, laptops and PCs. Which repairs are possible depends on the device model and the existing defect.

Can I send in my device from anywhere in Switzerland?

Yes. Upon prior agreement, repair devices can also be sent in from other regions of Switzerland.

Is a cost estimate free?

A short preliminary examination or an initial cost estimate is generally free of charge. A detailed technical examination or a written diagnostic report may be subject to a fee. We will inform you about this in advance.

What guarantee do I receive for a repair?

Mujitech provides a six-month voluntary warranty on replaced hardware components. The applicable conditions can be found in our General Terms and Conditions.

Can I come by without an appointment?

No. Mujitech does not have a public storefront. Personal device handovers are only possible after making an appointment in advance.
