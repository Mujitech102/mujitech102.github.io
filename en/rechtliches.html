Data protection and visitor measurement

If you contact us, we will use the information provided to process your request and coordinate the order. Online forms are submitted via FormSubmit to info@mujitech.ch. Please do not transmit passwords or particularly sensitive data. Alternatively, you can contact us directly by email or telephone. Form service information: FormSubmit data protection.

Hosting

This website is provided via GitHub Pages. When accessing, technical connection data is collected by the hosting provider. Further information can be found in the GitHub privacy policy.

Optional statistics with Google Analytics

We only load Google Analytics if you allow statistics cookies. Page views, scroll depth, contact clicks and form interactions are measured to improve the website. Form content, names, email addresses, phone numbers and login details are not sent to Analytics by our measurement code. URL queries and fragments are removed from the measured page addresses. Ad personalization and Google Signals are disabled in the website code. Google may process technical connection and usage data; processing abroad is possible. Information: Google data protection.

You can reject the statistics at any time or change your choice . Your selection is saved locally in the browser. If you revoke your consent, accessible analytics cookies will be deleted and measurement will be deactivated.
