Data protection and visitor measurement

Enquiry assistant using Tally

We use Tally BV, Belgium, for the contact step of our enquiry assistant. Opening this step loads the embedded form from Tally and involves technical connection data. Your selected request and the contact and device details you enter are stored through Tally and sent to Mujitech to process your enquiry. We receive a notification at info@mujitech.ch. According to the provider, form data is stored in Europe; Tally may use SendGrid in the United States for email notifications. See Tally privacy notice and data processing and subprocessors. Our measurement code does not send form answers to Analytics. Please do not submit passwords or particularly sensitive information. You can also contact us directly by email or phone. For access, correction or deletion of your enquiry details, contact info@mujitech.ch.

Tally's data processing agreement describes safeguards for relevant third-country transfers, including EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Details are available in the linked agreement. Tally data processing agreement

Contact requests

If you contact us, we will use the information provided to process your request and coordinate the order. Please do not transmit passwords or particularly sensitive data. Alternatively, you can contact us directly by email or telephone.

Hosting

This website is provided via GitHub Pages. When accessing, technical connection data is collected by the hosting provider. Further information can be found in the GitHub privacy policy.

GitHub may process technical data in the US, among other locations, and identifies Standard Contractual Clauses and the Data Privacy Framework as safeguards. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Optional statistics with Google Analytics

We load Google Analytics only if you allow statistics. Page views, scroll depth, contact clicks and use of the device price lookup are measured to improve the website. Our measurement code does not send form contents, names, email addresses, phone numbers or login details to Analytics. Free URL queries and fragments are removed from measured page addresses.

Separate consent for ad measurement

If you additionally choose “Statistics + ad measurement”, Google Analytics and Google Ads can link an ad click to a later enquiry. Correctly formatted Google click identifiers (GCLID, GBRAID or WBRAID) are then included in measured page addresses and ad-measurement cookies are allowed. Without this separate choice, the corresponding consent signals remain denied. Existing statistics choices are not expanded. Personalised advertising remains disabled. Google may process technical connection and usage data, including abroad. Google privacy.

You can change your choice at any time using . Your choice is stored locally in the browser. Switching to “Statistics only” deletes accessible ad-measurement cookies. Choosing “Necessary only” also deletes accessible Analytics cookies and disables measurement.

How we use your information

We use your information to answer your enquiry, prepare a quotation and carry out an agreed order.

Legal bases where the GDPR applies

Article 6(1)(b) GDPR applies to handling your contract-related enquiry and preparing and performing a contract with you, to the extent that the processing is necessary for those purposes.

For business contacts and the secure provision of the website, we rely on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interests are reliable business communication and the secure operation of the website. Where necessary for establishing or defending legal claims, protecting our rights is also a legitimate interest.

Optional analytics and advertising measurement are based on your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. Article 6(1)(c) GDPR applies to retention required by law where the conditions of Article 6(3) GDPR are met.

How long information is retained

The relevant criterion is how long the information is needed to handle your enquiry or order. Retention beyond that period is considered only to the extent required by statutory retention obligations or the establishment or defence of legal claims.

Your rights

Where the legal conditions are met, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. To exercise them, contact info@mujitech.ch.

Your right to object

Where the GDPR applies, you may object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interests. Send your objection to info@mujitech.ch.

Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw your consent to optional analytics and advertising measurement at any time through the existing cookie settings, with effect for the future. This does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Data protection supervision

You may report a suspected data protection violation to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC). Where the GDPR applies, you may lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority in the EEA, particularly in the place of your habitual residence, your place of work or the place of the alleged infringement.

Voluntary information

Providing information for your enquiry is voluntary. However, we cannot answer your enquiry without the contact details necessary for a reply.

Free diagnosis. Agree repair costs first.

We check the fault and discuss the possible repair and estimated cost with you. This assessment is free. It does not include a written report for your insurer. Only if you want written documentation for your insurer: on request, we prepare a report with a condition and damage description, photos and a repair-cost estimate for CHF 49. Standard diagnosis remains free. Complex additional investigations require a prior quotation and your approval. Please check with your insurer beforehand whether it accepts the report and covers the fee.

Shipping to us and free return delivery

You pay for shipping to Mujitech. Mujitech pays return shipping after the repair. Please wait until we confirm the shipping address before sending your device.
