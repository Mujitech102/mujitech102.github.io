Data protection and visitor measurement

Contact requests

If you contact us, we will use the information provided to process your request and coordinate the order. Online forms are submitted via FormSubmit to info@mujitech.ch. Please do not transmit passwords or particularly sensitive data. Alternatively, you can contact us directly by email or telephone. Form service information: FormSubmit data protection.

Hosting

This website is provided via GitHub Pages. When accessing, technical connection data is collected by the hosting provider. Further information can be found in the GitHub privacy policy.

Optional statistics with Google Analytics

We load Google Analytics only if you allow statistics. Page views, scroll depth, contact clicks and form interactions are measured to improve the website. Our measurement code does not send form contents, names, email addresses, phone numbers or login details to Analytics. Free URL queries and fragments are removed from measured page addresses.

Separate consent for ad measurement

If you additionally choose “Statistics + ad measurement”, Google Analytics and Google Ads can link an ad click to a later enquiry. Correctly formatted Google click identifiers (GCLID, GBRAID or WBRAID) are then included in measured page addresses and ad-measurement cookies are allowed. Without this separate choice, the corresponding consent signals remain denied. Existing statistics choices are not expanded. Personalised advertising remains disabled. Google may process technical connection and usage data, including abroad. Google privacy.

You can change your choice at any time using . Your choice is stored locally in the browser. Switching to “Statistics only” deletes accessible ad-measurement cookies. Choosing “Necessary only” also deletes accessible Analytics cookies and disables measurement.

Your concerns

For information, correction or deletion of the information you have provided to us, please contact info@mujitech.ch. Order data is used as part of processing and required storage.

Free diagnosis. Agree repair costs first.

We check the fault and discuss the possible repair and estimated cost with you. This assessment is free. It does not include a written report for your insurer. Only if you want written documentation for your insurer: on request, we prepare a report with a condition and damage description, photos and a repair-cost estimate for CHF 49. Standard diagnosis remains free. Complex additional investigations require a prior quotation and your approval. Please check with your insurer beforehand whether it accepts the report and covers the fee.

Shipping to us and free return delivery

You pay for shipping to Mujitech. Mujitech pays return shipping after the repair. Please wait until we confirm the shipping address before sending your device.
