Repair service for the Swiss Lake Constance region

From the Swiss Lake Constance region, you can arrange drop-off in Rorschach or discuss mail-in repair from within Switzerland. This does not mean drop-off is available in every lakeside town. We agree shipping and return arrangements for your enquiry.

From your enquiry to the return of your device

1. Describe the model and fault

Include the manufacturer, exact model and symptoms: for example a cracked screen, short battery life, charging trouble or a laptop that will not start. The model number, previous repairs and whether the picture and touch still work are helpful. Do not send passwords or PINs.

2. Arrange handover personally

Tell us your preferred location: St. Gallen, Zurich or Rorschach. We check the repair options and confirm the exact place and time. An enquiry is non-binding; please do not arrive without an agreed appointment.

3. Approve the assessment, parts and cost

Standard diagnosis is free. The price depends on the model, damage and replacement part option. Published model prices help you choose; “On request” is not a confirmed fixed price. We agree part quality, any parts order and the timetable before paid work. Repairs start only after your approval.

4. Agree how your device will be returned

We arrange the return personally. Return at the drop-off location is not automatically included. For mail-in repairs, you pay outbound shipping; Mujitech pays return shipping after repair. Confirm the shipping address before sending anything.

Questions about device drop-off

Can I turn up in Zurich or Rorschach without an appointment?

No. There is no publicly staffed Mujitech drop-off counter there. We agree the exact handover place and time personally for your appointment.

Which devices and faults can I enquire about?

Smartphones, tablets, laptops and desktop PCs. For iPhone and Samsung repairs, the exact model and symptoms help identify suitable parts. We confirm repair feasibility after assessment; different faults may need different solutions.

Which part quality and warranty apply?

Available part options depend on the model and are discussed with you. Mujitech is an independent repair provider. Replaced hardware has a voluntary 6-month warranty under the repair terms and exclusions.

What should I do before handover?

Back up your data if possible. If this is not possible, tell us first. Data preservation cannot be guaranteed. Contact us before shipping a swollen or unusually hot battery; do not send it without clarification.

Can Mujitech collect my device from my home?

Drop-off in the three locations is separate from personal collection at your address. Collection is only by prior arrangement; any travel surcharge is agreed in advance.

Are contact hours also drop-off hours?

No. You can reach Mujitech daily 08:00–20:00, Swiss time. Your handover appointment requires separate personal confirmation.
