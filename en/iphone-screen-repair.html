Screen replacement: picture, touch and glass

Cracked glass, black patches, lines and unresponsive touch can indicate different faults. Tell us whether the device still displays a picture and whether touch works. A dark screen alone does not prove that the display is faulty: the power supply or other components may be involved.

Depending on the model, the iPhone list distinguishes several screen variants. An iPhone screen price is only unambiguous together with the exact model and chosen part grade.

Choosing the screen quality

LCD is an economical screen technology. When used instead of OLED, colours and contrast can differ. Soft OLED uses a flexible OLED panel and is a replacement part, not an original device-maker screen. Original Service Pack and Original Pulled are separate variants. Premium is a replacement quality category; the exact technology must be confirmed for your model.

How your repair works

  1. Describe the model and fault and send a repair enquiry.
  2. Mujitech confirms the part, availability, price and agreed process before paid work begins.
  3. Send the device by arrangement or hand it over at an agreed appointment. You pay shipping to Mujitech; return shipping after repair is paid by Mujitech.

Before handing over your device

Back up your data where possible. Do not send passwords or unlock codes through the chat. Additional damage and other work are agreed with you in advance.

Common questions

Is replacing only the glass always cheaper?

A glass-only repair is not possible for every fault or model. A price for the complete screen assembly is not a quote for glass-only repair. Mujitech checks the appropriate option for your model and damage.

How long does replacement take?

Timing depends on the model, fault and parts availability. Mujitech personally confirms appointments and completion times before the order.
